# Blackjack

A classic Blackjack game with beautiful visuals and smooth animations.

## Features:
- Classic Blackjack gameplay
- Beautiful card designs with animations
- Betting system with quick bet buttons
- Game statistics tracking
- Responsive design for mobile and desktop

## How to Play:
1. Set your bet amount using the controls
2. Click "Deal Cards" to start
3. Choose to Hit, Stand, or Double Down
4. Try to get as close to 21 as possible without going over!

## Rules:
- Face cards (J, Q, K) are worth 10
- Aces are worth 11 or 1 (whichever is better)
- Dealer must hit until reaching 17 or higher, then must stand
- Blackjack (21 with 2 cards) pays 2:1

### Double Down:
- Available only when you have exactly 2 cards
- Doubles your bet amount
- You receive exactly one more card
- After double down, you automatically stand (cannot hit again)
- Requires sufficient balance (2× your current bet)

### Tie (Push):
- When both player and dealer have the same hand value (and neither busts)
- Your original bet is returned (no win, no loss)
- Balance remains unchanged
- Counts as a game played but doesn't affect win/loss statistics
