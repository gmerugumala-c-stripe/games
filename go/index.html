How to Play

💡 Beginner Tip

Hover over stones to see their liberties. Groups with only 1 liberty are in danger!

🎯 Objective

Control more territory than your opponent by placing stones and capturing enemy groups.

📍 Placement

Players alternate placing stones on the intersections of the grid lines. Black always moves first.

🔗 Liberties

A liberty is an empty point directly adjacent (not diagonal) to a stone. Groups need at least one liberty to survive.

⚔️ Capturing

Surround opponent's stones to capture them. When a group has no liberties left, it's removed from the board.

🚫 Suicide Rule

You cannot place a stone where it would have no liberties, unless that move captures opponent stones.

♻️ Ko Rule

You cannot immediately recapture a single stone if doing so would recreate the previous board position.

⏭️ Passing

Click "Pass" if you don't want to make a move. When both players pass consecutively, the game ends.

🏆 Scoring

Territory: Empty intersections surrounded by your stones
Captures: Opponent stones you've removed
Komi: White gets 6.5 bonus points
